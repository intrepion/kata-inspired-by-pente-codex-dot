# Tide & Stone
A game of lines and captured pairs played by Pearl and Ink.

## Language
**Intersection**: A crossing where one stone may be placed. _Avoid_: square, tile.
**Pair**: Two adjacent opponent stones bracketed by the mover's stones. _Avoid_: double.
**Capture**: Removal of a bracketed pair caused by a newly placed stone.
**Ply**: One accepted stone placement by one player. _Avoid_: round.
**Round**: A human move and the responding AI move.
**Overline**: A continuous line longer than five stones; also a winning line.
**Tournament opening**: Pearl starts at center and its second move is outside the central five-by-five area.
